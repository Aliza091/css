About the Uni Buddy Finder

Uni Buddy Finder is an online platform designed to help connect students in the Computing Systems module at Ulster University who share similar study styles and schedules, making group work and collaborative learning much easier and more effective.

Developed as part of Computing Systems coursework, this platform allows students to input their name, email, modules, and preferred study times to simplify forming study groups.

This tool helps students match with study buddies based on shared academic preferences. Designed by Aliza, Tara, and Khum.
